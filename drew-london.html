Direction

We like to let our work shine but showing the key elements of Drew London: integrity, kindness and inclusivity. Through colour, typography, illustration and fantastic content design by, Kate Lewis, we achieved a refreshed and revitalised identity for Drew, one that we felt showed off both our heart and our talents!

Illustration

“The Crowd” as I like to call them, populate the website to keep you company as you explore - their clothing and hair are transparent so they integrate and adapt to the background, picking up the different palette colours.

Inclusivity and representation are very important to us, so the eleven characters come in a few shapes and sizes - as Drew grows The Crowd will too!

Advertising & Results

The site and brand redesign won the agency several new clients within the first month of launch and the Facebook ad campaign had a 97% click rate, with website visitors staying for 9 minutes on average.
